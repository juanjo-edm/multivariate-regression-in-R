- Main slides: warm paper #F7F4EE.
- Section slides: deep ink #16232E.
- Proof panels: white #FFFFFF with hairline borders only when containment is needed.

Typography:
- Claims: Aptos Display.
- Body and labels: Aptos.
- Code labels: Aptos Mono.

Palette:
- Deep ink: #16232E
- Slate: #2C3E50
- Paper: #F7F4EE
- White: #FFFFFF
- Copper accent: #D95F0E
- Signal blue: #2166AC
- Muted green: #2D6A4F
- Alert red: #B2182B
- Hairline: #D9D0C2

Chart grammar:
- Use direct labels, no heavy legends.
- Use copper for the selected/winning model.
- Use slate and muted blue for comparison series.

Diagram grammar:
- Left-to-right process flows.
- Direction arrows only where the direction matters.
- Equal-role nodes share identical size, padding, and label treatment.

Footer and page markers:
- Small source footer on lower left.
- Slide number on lower right.

Banned motifs:
- Decorative blobs, generic card grids, pseudo-logos, mascot marks, and dense screenshots.
